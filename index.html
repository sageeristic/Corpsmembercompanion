GET HELP SAFELY

Help & Safety

Know which channel to try and what information to prepare before reporting a problem.

🆘

Immediate danger

Move to a safer public place if possible. Contact local emergency services, trusted people nearby, and relevant security authorities. Do not confront a threatening person alone.

Prepare: location, what happened, time, injuries, and a safe callback method.

🏢

NYSC administrative issue

Start with the appropriate camp official, platoon officer, LGI, ZI or state secretariat, depending on the stage and issue. Ask for the correct procedure and required documents.

Keep: call-up details, state code, dated copies, receipts and written responses. Share sensitive identifiers only through trusted official channels.

🏥

Illness or mental distress

Seek qualified medical care for urgent symptoms. Tell an appropriate camp official, supervisor or trusted person if illness affects participation. Do not rely on this app for diagnosis or treatment.

If you may harm yourself or someone else, seek immediate in-person help from emergency services or a trusted person.

💳

Allowance or financial trouble

Check official announcements and your payment details; ask the designated NYSC office what to verify. Keep dates and evidence, but never share PINs, passwords or one-time codes.

Avoid loan scams, “processing fee” requests and anyone promising guaranteed payment or posting changes.

Report an issue: preparation notes

Use this template when contacting an official. Do not include passwords or banking PINs.

Scam warning: Do not pay unofficial agents for redeployment, clearance, certificate collection or guaranteed posting. Verify any payment request with the relevant official office.

KEEP YOUR PAPERWORK TIDY

My Records

Store non-sensitive notes about documents, submissions and follow-ups. Avoid entering bank details, passwords or full identity numbers.

Privacy: information is stored locally in your browser, not sent to a server. Anyone who can unlock this device or access this browser profile may be able to view it. Export backups carefully and protect the files.
